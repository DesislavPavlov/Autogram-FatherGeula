# 47 - THE STILL SMALL VOICE
Pillar: Discipline (The Rule) · Idea: Claude — silence as the hardest discipline: a man keeps something in his ears from waking to sleep, not because he loves noise but because of what he hears when it stops; Elijah on Horeb, where the LORD was not in the wind, the earthquake or the fire, but in a still small voice; 1 Kings 19:12, Psalm 46:10

## Script (exactly this goes to ElevenLabs)
<<<SCRIPT
[conversational] You wake up, and the first thing you do is put something in your ears. A podcast in the shower. Music on the walk. A video while you eat. And at night, something playing so you can fall asleep.

[dry] Brother… when was the last time you heard nothing at all?

[conversational] Of everything I practise, the cold, the iron, the fasting… the hardest is silence. I am not joking. [pause] The cold only hurts. Silence shows you who you are.

[slowly] That is why you fill it. Not because you love the noise. Because of what you hear when it stops.

[conversational] There is a man in Scripture who knew all about running. Elijah. Exhausted, running for his life, sleeping in a cave on the mountain of God. And the Lord passed by.

[grave] A great and strong wind, breaking the rocks in pieces. But the Lord was not in the wind. Then an earthquake. He was not in the earthquake. Then a fire. He was not in the fire.

[reverent] "And after the fire a still small voice." [pause] First Kings nineteen.

[conversational] Not in the loudest thing. In the quietest. And a man with something in his ears every waking hour… will never hear it.

[slowly] I will be honest with you. When you first sit in silence, what comes up is not peace. It is everything you have been drowning out. Let it come. [pause] And if what comes is darker than you can carry, do not carry it alone. Tell a priest. A doctor. A brother.

[reverent] "Be still, and know that I am God." [pause] Psalm forty-six.

[intense] So tomorrow, before the first sound goes in… sit for a few minutes with nothing. No phone in your hand. Just you, and the quiet.

[intensely] The voice you need is not loud, brother. [pause] Turn the world down. Then listen.
SCRIPT>>>
