# 42 - ISN'T FAITH A CRUTCH FOR WEAK MEN
Pillar: Hard Questions · Idea: Claude — "Isn't faith just a crutch for weak men?" He agrees: every man leans on something; Paul boasted of weakness (2 Corinthians 12:9–10); the shame is not the crutch but limping and calling it strength.

## Script (exactly this goes to ElevenLabs)
<<<SCRIPT
[conversational] "Faith is a crutch for weak men." [pause] You have heard it. Maybe you have said it.
[dry] I am a priest, so you are waiting for me to argue. I am not going to.
[slowly] It is true. Christianity is for weak men.
[conversational] And I am not the first to say it. Saint Paul said it about himself — a man who was beaten with rods, stoned, shipwrecked three times… and who still chose to boast about his weakness, not his strength.
[reverent] "My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness." [pause] Second Corinthians twelve.
[conversational] So let me ask you something back, brother. What is the alternative?
[dry] The man who leans on nothing. [pause] I have met him. He leans on his phone. His image. His mood that morning. He just does not call them crutches.
[grave] Every man leans on something. The only question is whether it can hold your weight.
[slowly] A crutch is no shame. It is what a man reaches for when the leg is broken. [pause] The shame is limping for years… and calling it strength.
[intense] So stop pretending. Tonight, on your knees, say it out loud. I am weak.
[intensely] "When I am weak, then am I strong." [pause] Admit the limp, brother. Then lean on Him.
SCRIPT>>>
